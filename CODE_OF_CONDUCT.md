# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We are committed to a welcoming, respectful learning environment. Be considerate, accept constructive feedback, respect differing experience levels, and focus discussion on improving the project.

Harassment, discriminatory language, personal attacks, publication of private information, and other disruptive conduct are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior through the repository's private security-reporting channel or to its maintainers. Maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary. Reports will be reviewed fairly and handled as confidentially as practical.
